## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a bank's aggregated payment settlement system for handling offline payments and split settlements. The system should have payment, merchant, account, and daily settlement modules. The payment section should aggregate all payment methods and provide a unified entrance. The merchant section should manage merchant information, the account section should record merchant transactions, and the daily settlement section should perform reconciliation and error handling. The system should establish a payment transaction guarantee mechanism, including payment timeout correction, refund retry, and daily reconciliation. Assign lavender blush to body backgrounds and pale violet red to main components.

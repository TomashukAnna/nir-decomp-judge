# ТЗ № 2620 — WebGen-Bench train_002621

## Исходное ТЗ

Please create a business-focused brand awareness landing page to showcase the company's products and services. The page should have features such as company introduction, product display, and contact information. Users should be able to browse the company's history, product information, and get in touch with the company through the contact information. The page should have a clear navigation menu to help users quickly find the information they need. Apply melon for the layout background and tomato for interactive elements.

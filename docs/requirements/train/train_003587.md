## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a 24-hour self-service car wash mini-program to display car wash store information and provide functions such as recharge and QR code scanning. Users can browse the store list, view store details, including address, contact information, washing positions, fee information, package information, and washing tutorials. Users can also recharge their accounts and use the QR code scanning function to enter and exit the store. Additionally, users can apply to join the franchise and log in to their franchise account. Define the background as gold and components as dark goldenrod.

# ТЗ № 3633 — WebGen-Bench train_003634

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a Farm Machinery Hero mini-program for farm machinery management and services. The mini-program should have functionalities for querying farm machinery information, renting farm machinery, and maintaining farm machinery. Users should be able to log in, browse farm machinery information, rent farm machinery, schedule maintenance services, and view maintenance records. Apply platinum to the canvas background and midnight blue to all visible components.

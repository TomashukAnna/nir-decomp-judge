# ТЗ № 326 — WebGen-Bench train_000327

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a short drama APP for watching and managing short dramas. The APP should have four modules: mine, theater, history, and wallpaper. Users can browse multiple dramas in the theater module, view watched dramas in the history module, and manage their personal center in the mine module, including functions such as recharging H coins. Users can recharge H coins to watch dramas. Select bisque for layout background and dark salmon for component details.

# ТЗ № 3838 — WebGen-Bench train_003839

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a digital collectibles issuance platform for issuing and managing digital collectibles. The platform should have functionalities for issuing digital collectibles, managing digital collectibles, and user transactions. Users should be able to log in, browse and purchase digital collectibles, view their own collectibles library, and conduct transactions. The platform should also have statistical and analytical functions, enabling real-time monitoring of platform traffic and transaction situations. Declare bisque as the global background color and dark salmon for all UI pieces.

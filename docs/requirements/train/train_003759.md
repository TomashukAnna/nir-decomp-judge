# ТЗ № 3758 — WebGen-Bench train_003759

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement an e-commerce website for food products to sell snacks, beverages, bakery items, honey, and pollen. The website should have functionalities for browsing products, adding to cart, checkout, and payment. Users should be able to log in, browse different types of food products, add selected products to the cart, view products in the cart, proceed to checkout, and choose from various payment methods, including credit card, PayPal, bank transfer, UPI, phone pay, Gpay, Amazon Pay, and debit card. Style all pages with a faint peach background and coral components.

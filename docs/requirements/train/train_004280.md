# ТЗ № 4279 — WebGen-Bench train_004280

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a Tianjin medical insurance Alipay mini-program for medical service appointment and payment functions. The mini-program should have functionalities for registration, payment, appointment, and cancellation of appointments. Users should be able to log in, browse available medical services, make appointments for needed services, cancel existing appointments, and make online payments. Implement styling with rosy brown for the background and brown for components.

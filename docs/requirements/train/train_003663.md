# ТЗ № 3662 — WebGen-Bench train_003663

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a queue ordering website to optimize the consumption environment and stimulate consumption potential. The website should have queue functionality, including public queue mode and regional queue mode, and consumers can choose the queue method according to their needs. The website should also have a weighted dividend function, and consumers can get a weighted dividend opportunity based on their consumption amount. Users can log in to the website, browse products, place orders, queue, get weighted dividends, and view their consumption records. Use medium aquamarine for the background and sea green for components.

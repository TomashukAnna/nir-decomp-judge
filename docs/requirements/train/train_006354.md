# ТЗ № 6353 — WebGen-Bench train_006354

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement an online learning platform for selling online money-making courses. Teachers can upload YouTube videos, and students can watch videos without registering. After registering, students can offer services to other students who cannot complete the course steps. Students can browse ads below or beside the video, click on them, and start chatting with the students offering services. Teachers can receive payments. Use faint gray as the default background and slate gray for component styling.

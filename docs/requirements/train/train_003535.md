## Исходное ТЗ

Please optimize the speed of an e-commerce website, with the goal of reducing the loading time to less than 3 seconds and improving the Google PageSpeed Insights score. The website should have good mobile responsiveness, ensuring that users can quickly browse and purchase products. The website should be able to load product images, descriptions, and other content quickly, providing users with a smooth shopping experience. Apply lemon mist to the canvas background and dark orange to all visible components.

# ТЗ № 1699 — WebGen-Bench train_001700

## Исходное ТЗ

Please develop a cross-platform informational app to provide information services. The app should have user registration and login functionality, ensuring the app is user-friendly and intuitive. Users should be able to register for an account, log in to the app, browse and search for information, and the app should provide clear navigation and information display. Choose light yellow for global background and dark khaki for all components.

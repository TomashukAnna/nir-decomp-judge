# ТЗ № 2858 — WebGen-Bench train_002859

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement an online shopping app for displaying and selling products. The app should have functionalities for product display, sharing, shopping cart, and ordering. Users should be able to browse products, share products with friends, add products to the shopping cart, and submit orders for payment. The shopping cart function should allow users to increase or decrease the quantity of products. The ordering function should include steps such as selecting a delivery method, entering a delivery address, choosing a pickup time, and making a payment. Set overall background to alice blue, then style components with cornflower blue.

# ТЗ № 3138 — WebGen-Bench train_003139

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement an online shopping mall website for selling products and managing orders. The website should have functionalities for product management, order management, product promotion, payment methods, shipping methods, member module, administrator module, order management module, product price management, and mall marketing. Users can browse products, place orders, make payments, check order status, and register as members to enjoy discounts. Administrators can manage product information, orders, members, and perform marketing promotions. Use a amber background and apply chocolate to the UI components.

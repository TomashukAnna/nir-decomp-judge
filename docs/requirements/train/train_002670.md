# ТЗ № 2669 — WebGen-Bench train_002670

## Исходное ТЗ

Please create a landing page with a contact form, featuring a clean and user-friendly design. The contact form should collect the visitor's name, email address, and specific questions or messages. After submitting the form, the user should be directed to a thank you page, and the information should be sent to my inbox. The page should be responsive and user-friendly across different devices and screen sizes. Apply theme using light cyan as background and teal for elements.

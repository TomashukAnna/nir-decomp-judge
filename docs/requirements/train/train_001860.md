# ТЗ № 1859 — WebGen-Bench train_001860

## Исходное ТЗ

Please implement a digital creation and blockchain integration platform to help creators and developers build immersive meta-universes and blockchain applications. The platform should have functionalities for creating and managing meta-universes, building and deploying blockchain applications, and managing digital assets and identities. Users should be able to register and log in to the platform, create and manage their own meta-universes, build and deploy blockchain applications, and manage their digital assets and identities. Apply plum to the canvas background and dark magenta to all visible components.
